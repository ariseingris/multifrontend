## Publishing gates

Confirm full article and sources; specialist review of biology; label prototype and third-party claims; check working CTA and accessibility; use only owned/authorized media; record reviewer and revision date. For news, distinguish event date from publication date and cite original source. A milestone announcement needs a real milestone, date and approved project statement. An external case must name the external company prominently and must never sit under “Khách hàng AquaSense”. Archive or correct stale claims; no automatic quarterly content or fabricated activity.

No content provides treatment instructions or species-independent water limits. Additional authoritative husbandry references must be checked when writing biological articles. Pricing, market forecasts and financial modelling are outside this pipeline.

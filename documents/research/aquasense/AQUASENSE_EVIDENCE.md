## Commercial interpretation

The [Innovasea solution page](https://www.innovasea.com/aquaculture/) connects monitoring with mitigation and feeding. [RYNAN's homepage](https://rynanaquaculture.com/) links water management and farm efficiency. These are COMPANY_CLAIM observations. They suggest business questions for AquaSense rather than proving a financial return.

The FAO article is useful industry context because it discusses production, trade and price pressure. Tonnes of shrimp cannot be converted into an aquaculture-IoT addressable market without farm counts, adoption, geography and per-customer spending assumptions. No CAGR, TAM, financial forecast or currency conversion was calculated.

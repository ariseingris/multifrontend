Common themes are hazard awareness, people, operational continuity and device management. Technical product selection remains accessible after the business explanation. Compliance language is frequent, but vendor marketing does not establish which laws or certifications apply to FactSafe.

## Buyer interpretation

PROJECT_ASSUMPTION: the primary buyer is an EHS/safety manager, with plant operations, maintenance, IT/security and procurement influencing selection. Operators need readable alerts and escalation instructions. Managers need accountability, fleet status, records and support ownership. Procurement needs service scope and a credible total-cost explanation. These roles are analyst interpretations, not researched local personas.

The business-value hypothesis is faster awareness and coordinated handling of environmental hazards, supported by usable incident records. A measured reduction in administrative effort could also matter. Neither fewer accidents nor guaranteed compliance should be asserted without FactSafe evidence and jurisdiction-specific review.

## Proposed landing-page sections

1. Hero naming the industrial setting and hazard-visibility problem, with proposed “Discuss your site” CTA.
2. Industry-specific problems: fragmented readings, unclear escalation and manual records. Validate prevalence with customers.
3. Benefits tied to a workflow: detect, notify, acknowledge, respond and retain evidence. State which steps actually exist.
4. Hazard coverage and deployment model: gas/environmental parameters, sensors, connectivity and integration boundaries.
5. Roles and response process: who sees an event, who acts and how the organization documents it.
6. Trust: model-specific test documentation, supported standards, data handling and service arrangements. Add certification badges only with applicable documents.
7. Customer evidence with baseline, observation period and named permission to publish. Clearly label simulated demonstrations until real evidence exists.
8. Commercial scope explaining devices, software, maintenance, training and support; FAQ and repeated consultation CTA.

Use calm, specific language. Borrow the principle of explaining protection and operational work before technical screens. Do not reproduce competitor testimonials or turn their incident outcomes into FactSafe claims. Blackline's device/month pricing provides a comparison dimension, not a FactSafe price recommendation.

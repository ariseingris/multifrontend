## Pricing and business model

The Blackline source is evidence of a device-based monthly service model. Starting service prices do not establish hardware acquisition costs, minimum contract length, tax, installation, communications add-ons or total ownership cost. Recheck currency selection and full terms before a commercial comparison. The source was readable through web open; no purchase/account action was taken.

[Dräger's safety page](https://www.draeger.com/en-us_us/Safety) exposes rental, service and training routes, but numerical rates were unavailable in the inspected page. [Honeywell's gas-detection page](https://www.honeywell.com/us/en/solutions/gas-detection) describes fixed/portable equipment and cloud/on-premises software; numerical pricing and measured customer outcomes were not collected.

## Evidence boundaries

The ILO estimate establishes global occupational harm context, not the share addressable by gas sensors or FactSafe. It cannot support a product mortality-reduction claim or a local revenue forecast. No market CAGR, regulatory penalty, compliance percentage, ROI or financial calculation was produced.

[Daviteq gas monitoring](https://www.iot.daviteq.com/measurements/gas) appeared in official-domain search results but direct opens timed out. F06 is UNVERIFIED / LOW. No indexed battery-lifetime or certification assertion is admitted into the numerical dataset. Model-specific certifications must be checked against actual documentation and intended deployment.
